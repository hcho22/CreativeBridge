In a big, green forest, there was a little bear named Toby. Toby was very curious about the shiny thing he saw up in a tall tree. He felt excited and wanted to climb up to find out what it was. Toby's heart raced with excitement as he wrapped his fuzzy brown paws around the rough bark of the towering oak tree and began to climb, one careful step at a time. His claws gripped the trunk tightly as he pulled himself higher and higher, his little legs trembling slightly but his determination pushing him forward, because that mysterious shiny object sparkled like a tiny star caught in the branches. When he finally reached the glittering treasure nestled among the jade-green leaves, Toby gasped with delight—it was a beautiful crystal prism hanging from a silver chain, and when the sunlight hit it just right, it scattered rainbows all across the forest floor like magic! Toby took the shiny prism and slid down the tree. He showed it to his friends, and they all played with the rainbow lights. They were very happy. As Toby held up the crystal prism in the warm afternoon sun, brilliant beams of color—fiery red, tangerine orange, lemon yellow, emerald green, ocean blue, and royal purple—danced across the forest clearing like living ribbons of light. His best friend Ruby, a fox with fur the color of autumn leaves and a bushy tail tipped in cream, jumped and spun trying to catch the rainbow spots on the ground, while Pip the rabbit with velvety silver-gray fur giggled and hopped through the colorful beams. Even Oliver the wise old owl, with his speckled brown and white feathers and golden eyes, swooped down from his perch and admitted with a chuckle, "In all my years in this forest, I've never seen anything quite so magical—you've brought wonder and joy to us all today, little Toby!" Toby felt very proud and happy. He decided to hang the prism high in the tree again so its magic could shine every day. All the forest friends promised to visit it together often, making every day a little more special.
